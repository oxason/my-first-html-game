Storm Island 3D

A 3D battle royale. Pick a season, ride the bus, glide down, loot, build and outlast the storm.

Pick your hero

Party

Pick Solo, Duos, Trios or Squads, then type your own code or press Random. Friends type the same code to join you and automatically get your season, mode and team size. With no code you play in the public lobby.
Default controls (you can change them below): WASD move · mouse look (click the game to capture the mouse, Esc releases) · click shoot · Space jump off the bus · 1 wall · 2 floor · 3 ramp · R edit (turn the wall you face into a doorway) · E chop trees for wood · Tab or mouse wheel swap weapon. Walk over gold chests to loot them. Phones: left half moves, right half turns and shoots, and on-screen buttons handle building. Players in the same season share one island, and bots fill the lobby.

Change controls

Click an action, then press the new key. Esc cancels. If the key is already used, the two actions swap. Saved in your browser.
